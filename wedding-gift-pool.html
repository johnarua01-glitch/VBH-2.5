Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is there a fee for using WeddingPool?

None. Zero fees, zero charges. 100% of every contribution goes directly to the couple. We believe love shouldn't have a transaction cost.

How does the couple receive the money?

Funds are held securely until the couple requests a withdrawal. They can transfer to their bank account at any time, as many times as they like.

Can I contribute anonymously?

Yes. You can tick "Anonymous" when contributing and your name won't appear on the public feed — though the couple will still see your message privately.

Is there a minimum or maximum contribution?

The minimum is ₦1,000. There is no maximum — give as generously as your heart allows. Large contributors are recognized with a special "Golden Guest" badge.

How long can a campaign stay open?

There's no time limit. Campaigns can run for weeks before the wedding or stay open for months. The couple decides when to close and withdraw.

What happens if the couple cancels the wedding?

If a campaign is cancelled, all contributors are notified and refunds are processed automatically within 5 business days. No questions asked.
